The artist behind the photographs

Hi, I’m Tabitha. I see the art in it.

In the big celebrations, the tiny expressions, and the beautifully ordinary things we might otherwise forget. Photography is how I hold onto those moments — and help you hold onto yours.

I’m a photographer, digital artist, marketer, and mother in the Greensburg area. I love finding the color, light, and feeling that make a photograph worth returning to. Sometimes that means a quiet family portrait. Sometimes it means a little storybook magic.

Tell me your story

One artist. Many kinds of stories.

Rooted in connection.

Made with imagination.

Memories as Art is where your life and my imagination meet. Alongside portraits and weddings, I create commercial images and help businesses tell their stories through photography, design, and digital marketing.
