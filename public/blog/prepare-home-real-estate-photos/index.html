Before the first photograph

A practical home preparation checklist for your next property listing.

Memories as Art · Studio notes

Preparing a property before a photography appointment helps the images focus on the space. Whether you are an owner or an agent planning a Greensburg-area listing, a simple walkthrough can reveal the small details that are easiest to fix before the camera is set up.

Start with the rooms that matter most

Walk through the main living spaces, kitchen, bedrooms, and bathrooms. Straighten furniture and bedding, clear distracting surface clutter, and remove items that you do not want visible in published listing photographs.

Check personal and identifying details

Look for mail, documents, family photographs, prescription labels, computer screens, and valuables. Decide what should be put away before the appointment. The person preparing the home is best placed to know what should remain private.

Make light and access predictable

Check that the rooms can be accessed and discuss any lighting issues with your photographer. Identify locked spaces, alarm arrangements, parking, pets, and other practical details before the appointment rather than working them out on arrival.

Give the exterior the same attention

Tidy the entryway, arrange outdoor furniture, and move items that distract from the property when practical. Let your photographer know about features you want included and any areas that are not ready.

Confirm the project details

Before booking, ask about the scope, image count, turnaround, file specifications, and permitted uses. If you need photographs for more than a standard property listing, say so. Add-ons and availability should be confirmed directly rather than assumed.
