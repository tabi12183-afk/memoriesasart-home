The Journal · Wedding notes

Make room for the moments

A starting point for planning your Pittsburgh-area wedding photography timeline.

Memories as Art · Studio notes

A good wedding photography timeline gives important moments somewhere to happen. It also leaves room for the day to feel like a celebration. Whether your wedding is in Pittsburgh, Greensburg, or elsewhere in Western Pennsylvania, begin with the events and people that matter most to you.

Decide what you want remembered

Are getting-ready moments important? Do you want time for portraits together before the ceremony? Is a particular reception tradition a priority? Write down a short list before choosing coverage hours. Your photographer can discuss how those priorities fit the day you are planning.

Account for the actual journey

If the ceremony, portraits, and reception are in different places, include travel, parking, and the time it takes a group to leave one location and arrive at another. Check the route near your wedding date rather than relying only on a quiet-day estimate.

Make family photographs easier to organize

Prepare a concise list of the groupings you want. Share names as well as relationships, and flag any family circumstances you want handled thoughtfully. A trusted person who recognizes everyone can be helpful when gathering the next group.

Talk about light and a weather alternative

The look of an outdoor portrait changes with the time of day, season, and setting. Discuss your ceremony timing, the available portrait locations, and an indoor or sheltered alternative. Venue permissions and access should be confirmed with the venue.

Leave breathing room

A schedule with every minute assigned is difficult to follow when something runs late. Allow pauses around transitions and make sure the day includes time with the people you invited. Your photographs should remember the celebration you lived.

When you inquire about wedding photography, share your date, venue, broad schedule, and priorities. Coverage, staffing, and delivery details can then be discussed specifically for your plans.
