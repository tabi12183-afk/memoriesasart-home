Wedding questions

Good things to ask.

Do you photograph weddings in Pittsburgh?

Yes. Memories as Art serves the Pittsburgh area as well as Greensburg and Westmoreland County. Share your venue and date so we can discuss availability and any travel details.

What do wedding collections include?

Please inquire for current collection details. Coverage hours, photographers, digital images, artwork, travel, and delivery expectations should be confirmed in your proposal before booking.

Do you offer smaller-wedding coverage?

Tell me about the celebration and the hours you have in mind. We can discuss current options and whether the coverage available is a good fit.

Can we see more photographs?

Explore the wedding collection here and the linked WeddingWire archive. When you inquire, ask about additional examples relevant to your plans.

How do we check availability?

Use the inquiry link and include your wedding date and venue. Availability is confirmed personally; browsing the site does not reserve a date.
