Behind Memories as Art

Rooted in connection.
Made with imagination.

I’m a photographer, digital artist, and storyteller in the Greensburg area. For more than a decade, I have photographed the people and connections that make a life meaningful. My approach brings together creative styling and the emotion of a real moment.

As a mother, I know how quickly a familiar expression becomes a memory. As an artist, I love finding the color, light, and feeling that make a photograph worth returning to. Sometimes that means a quiet family portrait. Sometimes it means a little storybook magic.

Memories as Art is the place where those ideas meet: your life, seen with care and made into something beautiful.

One artist. Many kinds of stories.

From family life
to the life of a brand.

Alongside portrait and wedding photography, I create images for businesses and work in digital marketing through PTBA Business Services. Each project starts with the same question: what do you want someone to feel, understand, or remember?
