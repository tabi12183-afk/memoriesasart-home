The portrait experience

This chapter deserves
to be remembered, too.

Your maternity photographs can be soft and simple, creatively styled, or centered on the people sharing this season with you. Bring your ideas, your favorite colors, and a sense of what you would love to remember about this time.

We will talk through wardrobe, location, who will be included, and a date that feels comfortable for you. Ask about maternity photography in the Greensburg area and how you can plan newborn photographs as the next part of your family’s story.

A few things you might wonder

Before your session.

Can my partner or children join me?

Let me know who you would like included when we plan. Maternity photographs can celebrate both your individual experience and the connection within your family.

Do I need a special dress?

A special outfit is an option, but the starting point is what feels comfortable and true to you. We can discuss color, fabric, and the style you have in mind.

Can we discuss newborn photos at the same time?

Yes. Mention your interest when you inquire so we can talk through timing and available options for both chapters.
