The portrait experience

A little less “say cheese.”
A little more you.

You do not need a perfectly coordinated family or a child who loves the camera to begin. Tell me about the personalities coming to your session, the photographs you are drawn to, and anything that would help everyone feel at ease. Together, we can plan a portrait experience that makes sense for your family.

Whether you are updating the family photographs on your wall or celebrating a new chapter, we will start with what matters to you. I photograph families in the Greensburg area and serve clients throughout Westmoreland County. Location and session details are arranged when we plan your photographs.

A few things you might wonder

Before your session.

What should we wear?

Start with comfortable clothing and a small palette of colors that work together. Textures can add interest without everyone wearing the same outfit. You can read the family wardrobe guide in The Journal.

Can we include our dog?

Tell me about your dog when you inquire so we can discuss the setting, handling, and whether a family or dedicated pet session is the best fit.

Where will our session take place?

We will discuss your location preferences and the look you want. Location access, the season, and the needs of everyone attending all help shape that decision.
