Whether you are dreaming of a portrait of your pet alone or a photograph with the whole family, we will begin by talking about their personality. Tell me about their comfort around new people, favorite rewards, and anything that will help us plan a relaxed experience.

A few things you might wonder

Before your session.

Can I be in the photographs with my pet?

Absolutely—mention the people and pets you would like included when you inquire so we can discuss the right plan.

Does my pet need to know commands?

Let me know what your pet is comfortable with. We will discuss expectations and handling before making a plan; there is no need to describe them as a perfect poser.

What should I bring?

Ask about preparation for your planned setting. Familiar rewards, appropriate handling equipment, and a helper may be useful, depending on your pet and the session.
