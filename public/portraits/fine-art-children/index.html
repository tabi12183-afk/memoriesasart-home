The portrait experience

A story only
your child could tell.

Fine-art photography combines a thoughtful portrait with an artistic point of view. Color, light, styling, and creative editing help shape a finished image that feels like a story. Some photographs are rooted in a natural moment; others explore an imaginative scene.

The photographs here show examples of past creative work, not a list of sets or animals available for every session. Tell me what sparks your child’s imagination and we can discuss a current concept, how it might be created, and what is included before you book.

A few things you might wonder

Before your session.

Are these regular photographs or edited artwork?

Fine-art portraits can involve artistic editing and composites as well as the original portrait session. The approach depends on the concept; we will discuss how your artwork would be created.

Can we recreate a specific past image?

Past photographs are inspiration. Sets, locations, props, and availability can change, so please inquire about a concept before assuming a particular scene can be repeated.

Do you photograph milestones, too?

Yes. Baby and children’s milestones are also part of the portrait collection. Tell me the age or occasion and whether you prefer a simple portrait or a more imaginative direction.
