Newborn photography · Greensburg & Westmoreland County

So very little.
So very loved.

The sleepy expressions, tiny fingers, and feeling of holding a whole new world in your arms. Newborn photographs turn this fleeting beginning into something your family can revisit.

The portrait experience

A gentle beginning.
A memory for always.

Newborn photography begins with a conversation about your family and the images you love. We can discuss a simple portrait of your baby, photographs with parents or siblings, and the creative details that feel right for you. Comfort comes first; every baby brings their own pace.

If you are expecting, reach out with your due date so we can talk about timing and current availability. If your baby is already here, you are welcome to inquire too. We will discuss what works for your little one rather than treating a particular age as the only chance for meaningful photographs.

When should I reach out?

You can inquire during pregnancy with your due date, or after your baby arrives. Ask about current availability and how timing is arranged.

Can parents or siblings be included?

Let me know who you would like photographed so we can discuss the right session and plan for everyone involved.

What should we bring?

We will discuss preparation for your specific session. Tell me about feeding, settling, siblings, and any special details you want to include so we can plan thoughtfully.
